# Mission

## What We Build

Brote is a personal companion for plant care. Its purpose is not just to store information or remind the user of tasks — it helps the user *enjoy* the process of caring for their plants.

The app brings together everything related to each plant in one place: history, care, tasks, photos, AI consultations, and supplies used — creating a simple, close, and pleasant experience.

The core unit is not the calendar, the chat, or the database. **It's each plant.** Every plant has its own space where its entire history lives: basic information, photographs, logbook, AI conversations, care calendar, watering history, light measurements, fertilizations, repottings, related purchases, and observations. The app builds a *living memory* of each plant.

**Promise:** *Care for plants like an expert, without needing to be an expert — enjoying the process.*

## Who It's For

Designed primarily for people who are starting out with plants. The app doesn't aim to teach botany academically — it accompanies the user so they learn while caring for their plants. Every interaction should leave the user feeling a little more confident than before.

The home screen answers a single question: **"What does my garden need today?"** The user should be able to open the app and know what to do in under five seconds.

Every time the user closes the app, they should think: *"I know what to do."* And, months later, when comparing photos: *"How beautiful it's been to watch them grow."*

## Principles

**Core Values.** The app must always be: **Close, Clear, Trustworthy, Relaxing, Useful, Optimistic, Honest.**

**The App Doesn't Manage Plants. It Cultivates the Relationship Between a Person and Their Plants.** This shift in perspective affects every design decision. This is not a task manager with a plant theme; it's a place the user wants to return to because it reminds them why they started having plants: to enjoy them.

**The AI Is a Permanent Companion.** The AI knows the complete history of every plant — previous photos, evolution, user notes, watering history, fertilizations, repottings, past diseases, location — enabling contextualized responses, not generic ones. It speaks like a friend who knows a lot about plants, always transmitting calm, never judging mistakes, never making the user feel guilty.

**Design Principles.** Before adding any new feature, it must answer yes to:
1. Does it make caring for plants simpler?
2. Does it reduce the user's uncertainty?
3. Does it help the user enjoy their plants more?
4. Is the interface still simple?
5. Can the AI offer a concrete action at the end?

**Visual & Emotional Style.** The app conveys calm, nature, and home. Visual inspiration comes from watercolor illustrations, earth tones, and natural greens. It aims to feel welcoming, like an illustrated gardening notebook. Opening the app should feel like entering a small digital garden — not opening a spreadsheet.

## What It Is NOT

- Not a task manager with a plant theme.
- Not a calendar-centric scheduler.
- Not a botany textbook or academic tool.
- Not a technical dashboard or spreadsheet.

The app must never be: **Complicated, Overwhelming, Annoying, Alarmist, Pretentious, Confusing, Unreliable.**

Plants should not generate stress. They should generate wellbeing. If a feature adds unnecessary complexity, it probably doesn't belong in the app.
